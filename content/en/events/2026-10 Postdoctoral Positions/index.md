The research group led by Professor Zhisheng Niu and Associate Professor Sheng Zhou in the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University focuses on next-generation mobile communication systems. The group is currently recruiting **two postdoctoral researchers**.

## Requirements

1. A rigorous and responsible approach to research, integrity, and a strong spirit of collaboration.
2. Candidates who are about to receive or have already obtained a Ph.D. degree in Information and Communication Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Cognitive Science and Technology, or a related field. Applicants should have a solid theoretical foundation and research experience in domestic or international research projects.
3. Applicants must be under 35 years of age.
4. Good English proficiency and a record of publications in international journals in relevant fields.

## Salary and Benefits

1. Compensation will follow Tsinghua University's postdoctoral salary policy. Specific terms are negotiable.
2. Postdoctoral housing is available on campus, with allocation subject to the university's relevant regulations and availability.
3. Postdoctoral researchers are eligible for the university's policies regarding school and kindergarten enrollment for children of faculty and staff with institutional appointments, as well as applicable policies for affiliated schools and kindergartens.
4. Outstanding candidates may apply for the university's postdoctoral support programs and the Shuimu Scholars Program. Please visit Tsinghua University's official postdoctoral website for further information.
