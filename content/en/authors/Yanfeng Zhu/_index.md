Yanfeng Zhu received his bachelor's degree in Communication Engineering from the School of Telecommunications Engineering at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications in 2003. In September of the same year, he began a direct Ph.D. program in Communication and Information Systems at the Department of Electronic Engineering, Tsinghua University, graduating in 2008. His research interests include channel coding, quality-of-service (QoS) assurance for Voice over IP (VoIP), and wireless broadband technologies.

Ph.D. Thesis: Resource Optimization and Management in High-Density Wireless Local Area Networks

Career: IBM Research Center, China
