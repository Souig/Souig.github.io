Menghsi Chen received his bachelor's degree from the Department of Electrical Engineering at National Tsing Hua University in Taiwan in 2009. He is currently pursuing a master's degree at the Institute of Communications Engineering at National Tsing Hua University. From September 2010 to January 2011, he participated in an exchange program at Tsinghua University in Beijing, where he joined NiuLab for research and study.
