Long Long received his bachelor's degree in Electronic Science and Technology from Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications in 2005. In August 2005, he began pursuing a master's degree in Communication and Information Systems at the Department of Electronic Engineering, Tsinghua University, graduating in 2008. His research interests include the integration of communication and broadcasting networks.

Master's Thesis: System Design and TCP Enhancements in Integrated Communication and Broadcasting Networks
