Zhi Zhou received his bachelor's degree from the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University in June 2006. In September of the same year, he began pursuing a master's degree at the Communication Networks Laboratory in the same department, graduating in 2008. He participated in the development of several testbeds, including those for ad hoc networks, sensor networks, and high-density wireless local area networks (HD WLANs). His primary research interests include wireless networks.

Master's Thesis: Joint Tuning of Device Parameters and Performance Optimization for High-Density Wireless Local Area Networks (Zhi Zhou)
