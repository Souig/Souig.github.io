Yao Hua received his bachelor's degree in Communication Engineering from Xidian University in July 2005. In September of the same year, he began a direct Ph.D. program in Communication and Information Systems at the Department of Electronic Engineering, Tsinghua University, graduating in 2010. His research interests focus on communication network technologies.

Ph.D. Thesis: A Wireless LAN Architecture with Inter-AP Cooperation

Career: Beijing National Railway Research and Design Institute of Signal and Communication
