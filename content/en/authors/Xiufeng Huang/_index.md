Xiufeng Huang graduated from Donghua Senior High School in Guangdong Province in 2014 and was admitted to the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University that same year. In 2018, he was recommended for direct admission to the Ph.D. program under the supervision of Professor Sheng Zhou and graduated in 2023.
