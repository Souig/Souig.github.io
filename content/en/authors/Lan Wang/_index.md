Lan Wang received her bachelor's degree from the Department of Electronics and Information Engineering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (formerly Huazhong University of Science and Technology, then known as Huazhong University of Science and Technology) in 2001. She subsequently pursued a Ph.D. at the Department of Electronic Engineering, Tsinghua University, graduating in 2006. Her research interests include resource allocation and management in multiuser orthogonal frequency-division multiplexing (OFDM) systems.

Ph.D. Thesis: Radio Resource Management and Quality-of-Service Control in Multiuser OFDM Systems

Career: Siemens Beijing Research Institute
