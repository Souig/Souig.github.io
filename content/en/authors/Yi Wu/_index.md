Yi Wu received the Ph.D. degree and bachelor’s degree from Tsinghua University in 2003 and 2000, respectively. His research interests include cross-layer joint optimization for wireless mobile communication networks, TCP over wireless networks, wireless data link layer transmission techniques, and wireless channel modeling and prediction.

Ph.D. Thesis: Cross-Layer Protocol Optimization for Wireless TCP Networks (Outstanding Doctoral Dissertation, Tsinghua University)

Career: Nokia China Research Center, Beijing
