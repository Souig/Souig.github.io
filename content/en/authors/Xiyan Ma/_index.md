Xiyan Ma graduated from the School of Telecommunications Engineering at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications in 2001 with a bachelor's degree in Communication Engineering. In September of the same year, she began pursuing a master's degree in Communication and Information Systems at the Department of Electronic Engineering, Tsinghua University. Her research interests included wireless channel characteristics and quality-of-service (QoS) control in wireless local area networks (WLANs). She received her master's degree in engineering in June 2004.

Master's Thesis: Polling-Based Quality-of-Service Differentiation in Wireless LANs

Career: China Telecom Group, Beijing
