Lan Wang received her master's degree in Communication and Information Systems from the School of Information Science and Engineering at Shandong University in 2002. In the same year, she began pursuing a Ph.D. at the Department of Electronic Engineering, Tsinghua University, graduating in 2006. Her research interests include multidimensional radio resource management in MIMO and OFDM systems.

Ph.D. Thesis: Spatial Resource Management in Multiuser MIMO-OFDM Systems
