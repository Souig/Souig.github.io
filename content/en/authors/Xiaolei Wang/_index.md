Xiaolei Wang graduated from Beijing Normal University Experimental High School in 2005 and was recommended for admission to the Department of Precision Instruments at Tsinghua University that same year. In 2006, he transferred to the Department of Electronic Engineering. In 2009, he was recommended for direct admission to the Ph.D. program and graduated in 2015.
