Kai Zhang received his bachelor's degree from the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University in 2002. In the same year, he began pursuing a Ph.D. in the same department and graduated in 2007. His research interests include mobility management in the Internet, particularly Mobile IP and fast handover techniques.

Ph.D. Thesis: Resource Allocation and Optimization of Wireless MIMO Communication Systems under Non-Ideal Conditions

Career: Hong Kong Applied Science and Technology Research Institute (ASTRI)
