Jingbo Dong received his bachelor's degree in Science from the Basic Science Program at the School of Sciences, Tsinghua University, in 2006. In the same year, he began pursuing a master's degree at the Network Theory Laboratory in the Department of Electronic Engineering, graduating in 2008. His primary research interests include wireless ad hoc networks.
