Tao Shu received his bachelor's and master's degrees from the Department of Electronic Engineering at South China University of Technology in 1996 and 1999, respectively. He received the Ph.D. degree from Tsinghua University in 2003. His research interests include resource allocation and management for quality-of-service (QoS) assurance in wireless networks, as well as queueing theory.

During his doctoral studies, his research focused on the following topics:

1. QoS negotiation mechanisms in multimedia CDMA networks.
2. Data rate allocation schemes for multi-code CDMA systems.
3. Power allocation in multimedia CDMA networks under imperfect power control.
4. Optimal detection ordering for successive interference cancellation in multimedia CDMA systems.

Ph.D. Thesis: Resource Optimization in Wireless Multimedia Networks under Imperfect Conditions (Outstanding Doctoral Dissertation, Tsinghua University)

Post-Graduation Position: Postdoctoral Researcher at Arizona State University (ASU), USA
