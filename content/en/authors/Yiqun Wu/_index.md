Yiqun Wu received his bachelor's degree from the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University in June 2006. In September of the same year, he began pursuing a Ph.D. at the Communication Networks Laboratory in the same department, graduating in 2012. His research interests include wireless sensor networks and cross-layer protocol design.

Ph.D. Thesis: Energy-Efficient Cell Zooming in Wireless Cellular Networks

Career: Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd.
