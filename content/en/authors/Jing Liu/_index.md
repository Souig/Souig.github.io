Jing Liu received her bachelor's degree from the Department of Electronics and Information Engineering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2002. In September 2002, she began pursuing a Ph.D. at the Department of Electronic Engineering, Tsinghua University, graduating in 2007. Her research interests include wireless network resource management.

Ph.D. Thesis: End-to-End Quality-of-Service Control for Real-Time Multimedia Services in Wireless LANs

Career: Department of Electronic Engineering, Shenzhen University
