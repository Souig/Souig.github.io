Jing Zhu was born in Nanjing, Jiangsu Province, China. He received his bachelor's degree from the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University in 2000. He received his master's degree in engineering from Tsinghua University in 2001 and earned his Ph.D. from the University of Washington in 2004. His doctoral dissertation was titled Layer 2 (LL/MAC) Performance Analysis and Improvement of Wireless Heterogeneous Networks. Since 2004, he has been working at Intel's Communication Technology Lab in the United States.

Master's Thesis: Research on Strategies for Improving TCP/IP Performance in Wireless Networks

Post-Graduation Destination: University of Washington, USA
