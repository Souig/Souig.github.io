Zhengteng Zhu graduated from Anshun No. 2 Senior High School in Guizhou Province in 2009 and was admitted to the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University that same year, majoring in Electronic Information Science. In 2013, he was recommended for admission to the laboratory’s master’s program and graduated in 2016 with a degree in Information and Communication Engineering. His research interests include communication system simulation.
