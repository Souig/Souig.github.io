Jinri Huang graduated from the Department of Electronic Engineering at Xiamen University in 2001 and was admitted to the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University that same year. He received his master's degree in Communication and Information Systems in 2004 and continued his doctoral studies in the same field in September of that year, graduating in 2008. His research interests include wireless network resource management and vertical handover between heterogeneous networks.

Ph.D. Thesis: Joint Subcarrier Allocation and Packet Scheduling for OFDM-Based Transmission Systems

Career: SK Telecom, South Korea
