Xiaobo Chen received his bachelor's degree from the Department of Electronic Information Science and Technology, College of Information Technical Science, Nankai University, in 2003. In the same year, he entered the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University to pursue a master's degree in Communication and Information Systems, which he received in 2006. His research interests include wireless network resource management and quality-of-service (QoS) assurance.

Master's Thesis: Design and Implementation of an Energy-Efficient Routing Protocol for Wireless Sensor Networks

Career: Beijing Research Institute, Huawei
