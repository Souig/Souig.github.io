Xiang Duan received the Ph.D. degree from Tsinghua University in 2004. He received his bachelor's degree from the Department of Information and Communication Engineering at Xi'an Jiaotong University in 1999. His research interests include resource management, scheduling algorithms, power control, congestion control, and wireless ad hoc networks.

Ph.D. Thesis: Utility-Based Radio Resource Management and Quality-of-Service Control in Wireless Networks (Outstanding Doctoral Dissertation, Tsinghua University)

Career: Research Institute of China Mobile
