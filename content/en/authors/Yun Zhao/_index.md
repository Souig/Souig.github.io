Yun Zhao received his B.E. degree from the College of Information Science & Electronic Engineering from Zhejiang University, Hangzhou, China in 2012. He received the M.S. degree in the Department of Electronic Engineering, Tsinghua University, Beijing, China in 2016. His research interests include mobile cloud computing, green wireless communications and mobility management in wireless networks.
