Vilius Benetis is visiting from Networks group of COM Research Center (link http://www.com.dtu.dk) in DTU - Technical University of Denmark (link: http://www.dtu.dk). He is a second year PhD student and his thesis is on Capacity analysis of multiservice mobile networks. Vilius received his Master degree in Telecommunications in 2003 from DTU and a Bachelor Degree in Computer Science from Kaunas Technical University, Lithuania. On spare time he is actively involved in international student organisations (BEST (link: http://www.best.eu.org), IEEE Student Branch). He likes bicycling, jogging, hiking, reading and travelling.
