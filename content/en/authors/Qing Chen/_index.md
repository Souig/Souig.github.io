Qing Chen received his bachelor's degree in engineering from the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University in 2003. In the same year, he began pursuing a Ph.D. in the same department, graduating in 2008. He participated in the design of an ad hoc network testbed. His research interests include wireless resource management in ad hoc networks.

Ph.D. Thesis: Cooperative Resource Management in Wireless Ad Hoc Networks
