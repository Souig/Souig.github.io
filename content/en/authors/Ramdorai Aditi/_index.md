Aditi Ramdorai is a visiting student from Hamburg University of Technology. At the Tsinghua Communication Systems and Networking Lab she is working in the field of Integrated Communication and Broadcast Networks. Currently she is in the final year of the Global Engineering Programme which includes M.Sc. in Information and Communication Systems from Hamburg University of Technology and an MBA in Technology Management at the Northern Institute of Technology. Aditi completed her Bachelors Degree in Computer Science and Communication Engineering from the University of Duisburg-Essen, Germany and High School from Mumbai, India. She has also worked in KDDI R&D Labs, Japan and IMST GmbH, Germany. Her hobbies include Yoga, reading and travelling.
