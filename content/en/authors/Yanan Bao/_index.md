Yanan Bao graduated from Ningcheng Senior High School in Chifeng, Inner Mongolia, in 2006 and was admitted to the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University that same year. In 2010, he was recommended for admission to the master's program and graduated in 2013. His research interests primarily focus on the convergence of communication and broadcast networks.

Master’s Thesis: Dynamic Multicast-Based Energy-Efficient Mobile Video Service Mechanism

Post-Graduation Destination: University of California, Davis (UC Davis)
