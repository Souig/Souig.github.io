Xu Zhang graduated from Beijing No. 8 High School in 2003 and was admitted to the School of Electronic and Information Engineering at Beihang University in September of the same year. He received his bachelor's degree in 2007 and his master's degree in 2010. In September 2010, he began pursuing a Ph.D. at the Department of Electronic Engineering, Tsinghua University, graduating in 2015. His research interests include 60 GHz wireless local area networks (WLANs) and energy-efficient RF chain selection in multi-antenna systems.

Ph.D. Thesis: Energy-Efficient Wireless Resource Allocation with Multiple Antennas

Career: Huawei Beijing Research Institute
