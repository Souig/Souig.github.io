Xueying Guo received her B.S. and Ph.D. degree in Electronic Engineering from Tsinghua University, Beijing, China, in 2011 and 2017. From October 2013 to October 2014, she was a Research Scholar at Computer Engineering and System Group, Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Texas A&M University, College Station, TX, USA. Her research interests include radio resource management in cellular networks, cyber-physical systems, and green wireless communications. She was the recipient of the Best Student Paper Award in the 25th International Tele-traffic Congress (ITC) in 2013.
