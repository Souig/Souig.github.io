Jie Xu received his bachelor's degree in Engineering from the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University in 2008. In September of the same year, he began pursuing a master's degree at the Communication Networks Laboratory in the same department, graduating in 2010. His current research focuses on wireless mesh networks.

Master’s Thesis: Routing Selection and Channel Assignment in Wireless Mesh Networks with Cooperative Communications
