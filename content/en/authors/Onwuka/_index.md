Elizabeth Onwuka was born in Nigeria. She received her first and second degrees from the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the Federal University of Technology (FUT) in Nigeria. In September 2000, she began pursuing a Ph.D. in Communications at the Department of Electronic Engineering, Tsinghua University and received the Ph.D. degree in 2004. Her research interests included emerging issues in the mobile Internet.

Ph.D. Thesis: Design and Performance Evaluation of a Scalable Micro-Mobility Management Architecture for Mobile IPv6 Networks

Career: Lecturer at a university of technology in Nigeria
