Xin Xia graduated from Tianjin Yaohua High School in July 1998 and was admitted to Tsinghua University. He received his bachelor's degree from the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University in July 2002 and began pursuing a master's degree in the same department that year, graduating in 2005. His research interests include the Mobile IPv6 protocol and its simulation, as well as communication technologies for Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS).

Master's Thesis: Research on Wireless Access Technologies for Internet-Based Intelligent Transportation Systems
