Song Liu received his bachelor's degree from the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University in 2000. He received his master's degree in engineering from Tsinghua University in 2003. After graduating in July 2003, he went to the WINLAB laboratory at Rutgers University in the United States to pursue his Ph.D.

Research Interests: Stratospheric communication technologies and quality-of-service (QoS) control in wireless local area networks (WLANs). 

Master’s Thesis: Research on Multiple Access Control Techniques for High-Altitude Platform Communication Systems (Outstanding Master’s Thesis, Tsinghua University)

Post-Graduation Destination: Rutgers University, USA
