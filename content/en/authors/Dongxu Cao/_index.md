Dongxu Cao received his bachelor's degree from the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University in June 2008. In September of the same year, he began pursuing a Ph.D. at the Communication Networks Laboratory in the same department, graduating in 2013. His research interests include the deployment and optimization of heterogeneous cellular networks.

Ph.D. Thesis: Energy-Efficient Heterogeneous Cellular Network Planning and Dynamic Resource Optimization

Career: The Second Overall Design Department, The Second Academy of China Aerospace Science and Industry Corporation (CASIC)
