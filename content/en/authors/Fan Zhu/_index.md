Fan Zhu received his bachelor's degree from the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University in 2004. In the same year, he began pursuing a Ph.D. in the same department and graduated in 2010. His research interests include performance analysis of wireless networks and service integration across heterogeneous networks.

Ph.D. Thesis: Analysis and Optimization of Power-Saving Mechanisms in Wireless Data Communication Networks
