Zexi Yang graduated from Beijing No. 4 High School in 2003 and was admitted to the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University that same year, where he majored in Electronic Information Engineering and graduated in 2013. His primary research interests include resource optimization in wireless multi-hop cellular networks.

Ph.D. Thesis: Dynamic Relay Station–Base Station Association for Load Transfer

Career: Yushuyan Information Consulting (Beijing) Co., Ltd.
